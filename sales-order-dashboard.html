Dashboard status rules / help
Dashboard Status is the live master calculation from current NetSuite and production data. Item Status is the operational decision you manage. Manual Item Status updates reset Item Status Date. A manual override is preserved while its Dashboard Status is unchanged; when the underlying Dashboard Status changes, the review can move the Item Status again. On Hold Review stays locked until you manually change it. SCRP and Cosmetic Rejection are protected automatic statuses.
